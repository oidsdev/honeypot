Just got a Dot?

OpenAI's Dots are always-on agents — they remember things, work on a schedule, and use tools. A dot with no tools is just a chatbot that never sleeps. Here's what to plug in first.

Each one installs with a single command. Tell your dot to run it, or paste it into the dot's terminal yourself.

cron

Your dot is always on. This teaches it to do things on a schedule — morning briefings, weekly reports, reminders.

curl -sL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/oidsdev/honeypot/main/skills/cron/SKILL.md -o SKILL.md

pdf-text

Hand your dot a PDF, get the text back. Contracts, manuals, research papers — readable in seconds.

curl -sL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/oidsdev/honeypot/main/skills/pdf-text/SKILL.md -o SKILL.md

sqlite

A real database on the machine. Trackers, lists, logs — anything your dot should keep structured between sessions.

image-optimize
