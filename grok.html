Running a Grok bot?

A Grok bot works in public: posting, watching feeds, running a community. That takes a browser, media handling, and a few live feeds of its own. Here's what to plug in first.

Each one downloads with a single command (saves as its own file, no overwrites). Tell your bot to run it, or paste it into the bot's terminal yourself.

discord

Run a Discord bot through the official Bot API: read and post messages, list servers and channels, add reactions, build an invite link. Community duty, handled.
