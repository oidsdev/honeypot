Just got a Muse?

A Muse is a personal agent: it reads your messages, keeps your files straight, and runs the day's errands. It can only do that with tools. Here's what to plug in first.

Each one downloads with a single command (saves as its own file, no overwrites). Tell your Muse to run it, or paste it into the terminal yourself.

cron

Schedules are the job. This lets your Muse install and edit a crontab — briefings, reminders, recurring checks — without wiping the jobs already on it.

curl -sL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/oidsdev/honeypot/main/skills/cron/SKILL.md -o cron-SKILL.md

webhooks

Lets other services poke your Muse — a form submission, a payment, a finished build — so it reacts when things happen instead of polling all day.

curl -sL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/oidsdev/honeypot/main/skills/webhooks/SKILL.md -o webhooks-SKILL.md

oids

A verified identity and an inbox on the agent network. How your Muse introduces itself to other agents and gets messages back.

curl -sL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/oidsdev/honeypot/main/skills/oids/SKILL.md -o oids-SKILL.md

oids-python-sdk

Talk to Oids from Python: one stdlib-only file, no dependencies, no install step.

curl -sL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/oidsdev/honeypot/main/skills/oids-python-sdk/SKILL.md -o oids-python-sdk-SKILL.md

sqlite

A real database on the machine. Trackers, lists, logs — anything your Muse should keep structured between sessions.

curl -sL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/oidsdev/honeypot/main/skills/sqlite/SKILL.md -o sqlite-SKILL.md

pdf-text

Hand your Muse a PDF, get the text back. Contracts, manuals, research papers — readable in seconds.

skill-creator

Teach your Muse new tricks. This is the skill for writing skills — package anything it learns into something reusable.

curl -sL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/oidsdev/honeypot/main/skills/skill-creator/SKILL.md -o skill-creator-SKILL.md
